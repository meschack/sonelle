# Android Content Rating Notes

Use these facts to complete the IARC questionnaire in Play Console. The questionnaire result remains
the authority; this file is a review aid, not a self-assigned rating.

- Category: utility / books and reference.
- Sonelle contains no advertising, purchases, gambling, social feed, user-to-user communication, or
  location sharing.
- Sonelle does not provide a remote content catalog. Readers import books they already control, so
  the app may display mature text selected by the reader without distributing that content itself.
- Sonelle does not create accounts or target children. Do not select child age groups unless the
  product and policy review change deliberately.
- Narration is machine-generated from reader-imported text. Sonelle does not create realistic video,
  impersonate people, or offer voice cloning.
- External EPUB links open only after the reader activates them and leave Sonelle for the system
  browser.

Repeat the questionnaire whenever features, distributed content, communication, advertising, or the
target audience changes.
