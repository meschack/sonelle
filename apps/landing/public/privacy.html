Privacy policy · effective August 11, 2026

Local means local.

Your library is intimate. Sonelle is built so reading it does not require handing it over.

01

What stays on your device

Sonelle stores imported books, reading progress, bookmarks, saved passages, prepared narration, preferences, and bounded error diagnostics in its local application data.

Sonelle does not upload your books or reading activity. There is no Sonelle account, advertising profile, analytics feed, or automatic crash-report upload.

02

When Sonelle uses the network

Offline voice files are downloaded only after you choose to install them. The file host receives ordinary connection information such as an IP address; Sonelle does not send your book text with that download.

Links inside a book open in your system browser only after you activate them. The destination then applies its own privacy policy.

03

Voice choices

Sonelle's standard offline narration processes book text on your device after its voice files are installed. Prepared audio remains local.

Device-provided voices are separate and optional. Before Sonelle uses one, it tells you whether the selected Android speech engine requires a network connection. A network voice may send the text being spoken to that engine's provider under the provider's terms.

04

Diagnostics and sharing

Error diagnostics are written locally and are never uploaded automatically. If you choose to share a report, review it first. GitHub issues are public, so do not include books, device identifiers, or personal information.

05

Control and deletion

You can remove downloaded voice files without deleting books or progress. Delete Sonelle's local data through Android system settings, or uninstall Sonelle, to remove its application data from the device. Backups controlled by your operating system follow the settings of that service.

06

Changes and contact

This policy changes when Sonelle's real data behavior changes, not as decorative legal confetti. The effective date above will be updated and material changes will be called out with the release.

Questions and corrections can be raised in the Sonelle issue tracker.
